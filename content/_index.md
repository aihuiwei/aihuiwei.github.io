I study how central bank communication moves financial markets. My work combines large language models with high-frequency event-study methods to measure how policy communication affects asset prices and trading activity.

I have interned at the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the Inter-American Development Bank (IDB). In November 2026, I will join the Hong Kong Institute for Monetary and Financial Research (Hong Kong Monetary Authority) as a Ph.D. research intern.
