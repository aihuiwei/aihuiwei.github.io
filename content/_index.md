I study how central bank communication moves financial markets. My work combines large language models with high-frequency event-study methods to measure how policy communication affects asset prices and trading activity.

I am currently a Ph.D. research intern at the Hong Kong Institute for Monetary and Financial Research (HKIMR). I have also held Ph.D. internships at the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the Inter-American Development Bank (IDB).
